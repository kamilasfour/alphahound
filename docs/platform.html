Overview

What is AlphaHound?

AlphaHound is a fully automated options trading engine. It ingests real market-intelligence sources every 15 minutes, scores each ticker across five independent pillars, and when enough of them agree on the same name and direction, it places a defined-risk options spread on an Alpaca paper account — without human intervention. The thesis is convergence: a single signal is noise, but stacked agreement across independent sources is an edge that surfaces before the move.

🎯
Multi-Pillar Convergence
Five pillars score every ticker. A SUPER SIGNAL fires only when the composite reaches 5.0+ with 4 or more pillars agreeing on a clear, non-neutral direction.
🧩
Defined-Risk Options
Trades bull and bear call spreads — capped downside by construction. No naked options, no short stock, no margin calls. Max loss is the debit paid.
🗓️
Catalyst-Aware
Signals are timed against a 7–60 day catalyst window — earnings, contract awards, binary events — so the trade has a reason to resolve before expiry.
🛡️
Hard Guardrails
Max 3 open spreads, max 2 contracts each, automatic stop-loss, time-based exits, and a portfolio-health halt that stops new entries when positions bleed.
🌗
Macro Gate
New positions open only when the market regime reads RISK_ON. NEUTRAL and RISK_OFF both block entry — standing down is a deliberate, correct outcome.
📊
Hit Rate Validation
Every trade resolves to a win/loss. The hit-rate gate must clear a threshold across enough resolved trades before any live capital is ever deployed.

Mission

The Goal

Prove a genuine, repeatable edge on a paper account first — then, and only then, deploy real capital. Every layer of the system is built around defined risk and disciplined entry: the engine would rather stand down than force a marginal trade.

Discipline over speed: Defined-risk spreads, a hard 3-position cap, and a RISK_ON-only macro gate exist to make ruin structurally difficult. The paper hit-rate gate is treated as a wall, not a formality — a clean, statistically meaningful track record has to come before any live capital.
Core Thesis
Independent sources converging on one name + direction → a tradeable edge before the market fully reprices
Congress buying, options desks sweeping calls, a fresh contract award, social velocity spiking, and a known catalyst ahead — when they line up, that agreement is the signal.

Core Algorithm

Each ticker is scored independently on five pillars. A pillar "fires" when its evidence is strong enough to contribute. The pillars are intentionally diverse — they pick up different kinds of informed activity, so agreement between them is meaningful.

Core Algorithm

The Convergence Score

The convergence scorer combines the pillars into a single composite score and a direction for each ticker. A SUPER SIGNAL — the only thing eligible for execution — requires all three conditions below to hold at once.

Super Signal Criteria
composite_score ≥ 5.0 AND pillars_fired ≥ 4 AND direction ≠ NEUTRAL
A high score from a single dominant pillar is not enough — breadth (4+ pillars) is required so the trade rests on genuine agreement, not one loud source.
Composite ScoreClassificationAction
< 2.5BackgroundIgnored — not surfaced
2.5 – 4.9Developing / WatchShown on the watch list, no execution
≥ 5.0 (with 4+ pillars)SUPER SIGNALEligible to execute — pending macro gate & guardrails
High-conviction, no-catalyst path: A super signal can also fire without a catalyst in-window if the score is strong (≥ 5.0), at least 4 pillars agree, and direction is non-neutral. These are capped to a shorter max hold.
Freshness: The scorer uses a pure NOT EXISTS check and signals carry their actual computed_at timestamp, so the executor always evaluates genuinely fresh signals rather than re-processing or stale ones.
Timing

Catalyst Window

Signals are timed against a catalyst window so a spread has a concrete reason to resolve before it expires. Trades without a catalyst in-window are held to a shorter limit.

ParameterValueMeaning
Catalyst window7 – 60 daysA binary event must fall in this range to count as catalyst-backed
Catalyst exit3 days after eventClose shortly after the catalyst fires — the thesis has played out
No-catalyst max hold14 daysHigh-conviction no-catalyst trades are time-boxed tighter
Expiry guard5 DTEAvoid holding into the final days of an option's life
Execution

Options Structures

AlphaHound trades vertical call spreads — defined-risk by construction. Direction determines whether it builds a bull or bear spread; the structure recommender sizes strikes from the signal and available chain.

DirectionStructureRisk Profile
LONGBull call spread (buy lower strike, sell higher)Max loss = net debit · Max gain = strike width − debit
SHORTBear call spread (sell lower strike, buy higher)Defined risk · Capped gain · No naked short exposure
NEUTRALNo tradeDirection must be non-neutral to execute
Golden rules — options execution: Alpaca leg format uses sell as the side with a separate position_intent field (not sell_to_open as the side). Options market orders are only accepted during market hours (6:30 AM – 1:00 PM PT), so stop-loss closes are queued for the open. Duplicate prevention cross-checks live Alpaca positions, not just the DB.
Not always executable: Some names are flagged NOT EXECUTABLE when the listed options chain is too short for the current price (e.g. a stock trading well above the highest listed strike). These show on the dashboard but are skipped with a reason.
Risk Management

Macro Gate

Before any new position, the engine reads the overall market regime from a basket of global instruments. The gate is strict: only RISK_ON permits new entries.

VerdictConditionEffect on New Entries
RISK_ONMacro score ≥ +0.20New spreads permitted
NEUTRALBetween thresholdsBlocked — no new entries
RISK_OFFMacro score ≤ −0.10Blocked — no new entries
SPY 5-Day Override
if SPY 5-day return < −2% → force RISK_OFF
A sharp short-term drawdown in the broad market overrides the basket and stands the engine down regardless of the composite macro score.
Note: NEUTRAL means no new trades — only RISK_ON allows entry. Standing down in an uncertain regime is intentional, not a missed opportunity. Market hours are determined by a time-based ET check rather than the broker's open/closed flag, which can report stale values.
Risk Management

Guardrails

Hard limits bound the risk on every trade and across the portfolio. These are not advisory — they block execution.

RuleValue
Max open spreads3 (hard limit)
Max contracts per spread2
Stop loss−80%
Gain target+50%
Expiry guard5 DTE
Catalyst exit3 days after the event
Time limit (no catalyst)14 days max hold
Macro gateRISK_ON only
SPY 5d trend override< −2% forces RISK_OFF
Portfolio health halt2+ positions >50% down = no new entries
Why these exist: Each rule caps a distinct failure mode — the 3-position limit and contracts cap bound total exposure, the stop-loss and time exits bound any single trade, and the portfolio-health halt stops the engine from adding risk into a book that's already bleeding. Together they make a runaway sequence of trades structurally impossible.
Risk Management

Exit Logic

The options monitor checks every open spread each cycle against the exit triggers below. Closes use the Alpaca positions endpoint directly, queued for market open when needed.

TriggerConditionAction
Stop lossSpread value down 80% from entryClose the position (queued for open if after hours)
Gain targetSpread up 50% from entryTake profit
Catalyst exit3 days after the catalyst event firedClose — thesis has resolved
Time limit14 days held (no-catalyst trades)Close on the clock
Expiry guardWithin 5 days of expiryClose to avoid end-of-life risk
Implementation note: Stop-loss closes use the Alpaca position-close endpoint (DELETE /v2/positions/{symbol}) rather than placing a counter-order, so an exit is a single atomic action.

Dashboard Access

The dashboard sits behind a single shared access code. The gate validates the code server-side and issues a signed, expiring session cookie; unauthenticated page loads redirect to the login screen and unauthenticated API calls return 401.

SettingDefaultOverride

Session secretbuilt-in fallbackDASHBOARD_SESSION_SECRET in .env

First-run walkthrough: On first login per device, a walkthrough opens automatically — concept first (pillars, convergence, gates), then a tour of the dashboard tabs. Replay anytime via window.AHWalkthrough.open().
Security note: A short numeric access code is fine on the internal VM over localhost. If the dashboard is ever exposed beyond the box, raise the code length via DASHBOARD_ACCESS_CODE, set a strong DASHBOARD_SESSION_SECRET, and serve over HTTPS (enable the secure cookie flag in auth.py).
Go-Live Gate

Hit Rate Validation

Everything runs on the Alpaca paper account until the hit-rate gate clears across enough resolved trades. This is the go/no-go wall before real capital is ever committed.

Hit RateDecisionNext Step
≥ 60%GO LIVEDeploy real capital with conservative sizing
55% – 60%CAUTIOUS GOGo live at reduced size, scale as more trades resolve
50% – 55%EXTEND PAPERContinue paper, diagnose weak pillars
< 50%STOPNo edge. No live capital. Diagnose and rebuild.
Statistical significance: A high hit rate on a handful of trades means nothing. The gate only counts once enough trades have resolved to be statistically meaningful — breadth of evidence matters more than any single streak.
