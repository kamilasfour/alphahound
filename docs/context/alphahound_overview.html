The thesis

Why this exists

Most retail trading systems are reactive — they alert you to something that already happened and ask you to decide. AlphaHound is different. It is a convergence detector: it looks for moments when multiple independent sources of signal all point in the same direction at the same time, with a binary event approaching that forces a resolution.

That convergence is rare. When it happens, the edge is real. The system's job is to find those moments automatically, every 15 minutes, across the entire market, and act on them before the window closes.

Core thesis
"When institutional options flow, congressional positioning, and social velocity all converge on the same ticker within 45 days of a binary catalyst — that is a non-random signal. We capture it systematically."
The engine

Seven independent pillars

Every ticker is scored across seven pillars every 15 minutes. Each pillar scores 0, 0.5, or 1.0. A non-linear boost of +1.5 fires when four or more independent pillars converge — because independent sources agreeing is worth more than their sum. A Super Signal requires a composite score of 4.0+ with 4+ pillars firing and a binary catalyst in the 14–45 day window.

P1 · SCALABILITY
Liquidity gate
Is this ticker optionable with tight spreads on a $50k account? Tier A watchlist = 1.0. Extended = 0.5. Unknown = 0.
Source: Internal watchlist
P2 · OPTIONS FLOW
Unusual Whales sweep
Multi-exchange options sweep with Unusual Whales sentiment classification. Unclassified flow does not vote on direction.
Source: Unusual Whales API
P3a · CONGRESSIONAL
STOCK Act disclosure
Congressional buy above $15k from a committee member relevant to the sector. Filed under STOCK Act within 45 days.
Source: Quiver Quantitative
P3b · GOV CONTRACT
DoD / DARPA / FDA award
News post containing anchor contract keywords: DoD, DARPA, MDA, Pentagon, IDIQ. Generic "approval" or "grant" does not fire.
Source: Finnhub, Substack
P3c · SOCIAL VELOCITY
Mention velocity +150%
24h post count versus 7-day baseline. Fires at +150% velocity (2.5x baseline). Moderate score at +50%.
Source: ApeWisdom, Substack
P4 · META-CONVERGENCE
Non-linear boost
+1.5 added to composite when 4+ independent pillars fire simultaneously. Independent sources agreeing is worth more than their sum.
Internal computation
P6 · CATALYST
Binary event 14–45 days
Earnings, FDA PDUFA action date, or FOMC within the window. Sweet spot 20–40 days. Required for Super Signal — no catalyst, no trade.
Source: Finnhub, FDA calendar
P7 · QUANT MOMENTUM
Flow + price alignment
Institutional flow polarity aligned with 5-day price trend. Score 1.0 when both agree, 0.5 when one is absent, 0 when they conflict.
Source: Sentiment scores, Polygon

Architecture

How it runs autonomously

The full pipeline runs every 15 minutes via Windows Task Scheduler. No human is required in the loop. The dashboard shows the current state at any time.

1

INGEST — every 15 min

10 adapters run in parallel: ApeWisdom, Finnhub, Unusual Whales, Quiver (congressional), Substack, Yahoo Finance, Kalshi, Massive (price), Earnings Calendar, Massive History. ~73k posts ingested per 24h.

2

SCORE — every 15 min

FinBERT sentiment scoring on all new posts. Institutional flow polarity computed. Velocity signals calculated per ticker. ~73k posts scored per 24h.

3

CONVERGENCE SCAN — every 15 min

All 1,025 active tickers scored across 7 pillars. Super signals identified (score ≥ 4.0, 4+ pillars, binary catalyst required). Structure plan auto-attached to every super signal. Results written to convergence_signals table.

4

OPTIONS EXECUTION — market hours only

Macro check (RISK_OFF blocks all entries). Already-executed check. Cash availability check. Structure retrieved from DB (no repricing). Order submitted to Alpaca paper API. Limit order at estimated debit +10% buffer for fill probability.

5

DASHBOARD — live at localhost:8080

Multi-Pillar Convergence view shows all super signals with full pillar breakdown, structure recommendation, and "View Structure Plan" modal. KPI strip shows macro state, super signal count, and P&L. Refreshes on every load.

Roadmap

Phase 1 and Phase 2

Phase 1 — Active now

Prove the edge

$50k → $150k
  • Paper trading on Alpaca with real signal logic and real market prices
  • Track hit rate on every super signal: did the trade make money?
  • Validate that 4-pillar convergence + catalyst has a positive expected value
  • Tune thresholds based on results: UNUSUAL_SCORE_MIN, catalyst window, pillar weights
  • Build options P&L tracking into the dashboard with actual fill prices
  • Close out positions at 50% max gain target automatically
  • Run for 60–90 days minimum before drawing any conclusions
  • Success = hit rate > 55% on super signals, Sharpe > 1.2 on paper
Phase 2 — After validation

Deploy real capital

$50k → $500k
  • Switch from paper to live Alpaca account with $50k real capital
  • Replace BS/HV30 pricing with live IV feed (Unusual Whales or CBOE)
  • Validate listed strikes against actual options chain before every order
  • Add position management: auto-close at 50% gain or 100% loss
  • Add IV rank filter — only trade when IV rank < 50 (options relatively cheap)
  • Add earnings surprise predictor from analyst estimate dispersion
  • Expand catalyst types: activist investor filings, merger arbitrage events
  • Scale position sizing with proven Kelly fraction as edge is confirmed
The key constraint

Phase 2 does not start until Phase 1 produces at least 30 resolved super signal trades with documented outcomes. Edge without evidence is gambling. The system is built to find edge — Phase 1 is how we prove it exists.
